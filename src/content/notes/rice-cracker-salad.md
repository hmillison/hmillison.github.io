Inspired by the incredible cookbook “a child of rice fields”. This is a mash up of two Lao/Isan recipes: eggplant salad (yum makeua yao) and rice cracker salad (naem pa). Traditionally the rice cracker salad is served with a smoked fish, so I figured the soft roasted eggplant would serve as a nice contrast to the crunchiness of the rice cracker.

## Notes

Using the store bought cracker is a great shortcut vs the painstaking process of deep frying rice yourself like you would do for naem khao tod.

## Ingredients

- 200g rice cakes ([I use this Japanese brand](https://www.sayweee.com/en/product/Amanoya-Himemaru-Rice-Cracker/98358))
- 2 lb eggplant
- 2 T lime
- 2 T Fish Sauce (or Soy if vegan)
- Optionally: 3 tbsp of a citrus jam (homemade with kumquat) or Korean yuzu cheong
- Optional: 100g chopped sour fruit (green mango, stair fruit, green apples, whatever else you can find)
- 30g coriander + 30g mint
- 50g fried shallots
- 50g spring onions
- 8-10 fried chilis

## Instructions

1. Roast eggplant until skin is fully blistered and eggplant is soft, peel and slice up eggplant into pieces.
2. Gently break up the rice cakes using a pestle. You want little chunks, not dust.
3. Mix everything and toss in dressing.
4. Serve with lettuce / perilla leaves.
